# E05.4 cross-route lifecycle and acceptance

Date: 2026-08-29
Status: complete

## Delivered

- Added one versioned 15-case acceptance corpus shared byte-for-byte by Rust, Swift, and
  Kotlin. It covers connect, failure, cancellation, backgrounding, reconnect, route
  switching, host-key mismatch, missing tmux, and revoked Device authority.
- Added matching pure decision engines to both native applications. Direct SSH owns its
  transport and optional remote tmux continuity; Device Sessions use Host-service replay
  and single-writer authority. Neither route replays terminal input.
- Made every terminal-ending event cancel and disconnect, clear pending input, preserve or
  discard output according to route policy, and release a Device writer when required.
- Added fixture-backed native tests to the existing route-contract gates and retained all
  E04 terminal conformance, interaction, acceptance, bounds, and writer-control checks.
- Repaired the canonical E04 fixture synchronizer after Android flavor removal and added
  the new cross-route corpus to its drift checks.
