Split & Canvas, reworked

A working mock of how the two workspace layouts could feel. Everything is interactive. The terminals are simulated: click one and type help.

Both views

One set of sessions, two views

Split and Canvas are two layouts of the same live panes. Switching animates each pane to its place instead of rebuilding the workspace. Nothing reconnects, and scrollback and focus carry over. The canvas can hold more than the split shows.

Needs-you queue

An agent waiting for approval raises a pill at the top of either view. Clicking it jumps straight to that agent, even from Split, where the agent has no pane.

Every action in ⌘K

Every split, layout, canvas action and "jump to session" in one palette, with shortcuts shown next to each so people learn them.

See where it lands

While you drag, every pane shows five drop zones and the half it will take lights up. The center swaps two panes. A full split says so before you let go.

Rearrange by dragging headers

Drag a pane by its header onto another pane to move or swap it. This is the most common way people fix a layout they got wrong.

Magnetic dividers

Dividers snap to ½, ⅓ and ⅔ and show the ratio while you drag. Double-click a divider to even out that split.

Zoom a pane

Temporarily fill the workspace with one pane. The others keep running. A pill shows what's hidden and how to get it back.

Layouts and Equalize

One-click layouts (columns, rows, main + stack, grid). They reuse the panes you have and open new ones only when needed. Panes that don't fit stay alive on the canvas. Cap raised from 4 to 6.

Keyboard all the way

Directional focus picks the nearest pane on screen, not the next leaf in the tree. Resizing moves the closest divider on that axis.

Obvious focus

Inactive panes dim, and the focused one gets an accent ring and a live cursor. Broadcast marks every pane it will type into and puts an amber rim around the workspace.

Snap and alignment guides

Nodes snap to an 8 px grid and to other nodes' edges and centers, with pink guides showing what they aligned to. Hold ⌥ to place freely.

Today: nodes move freely, with no grid or guides
Readable when zoomed out

Below 55% zoom, each terminal becomes a card showing its name, status and latest output line, sized to stay readable. You can watch a whole fleet at once.

Scroll does the right thing

A two-finger scroll pans and a pinch zooms at the cursor. Over the focused terminal, scroll moves its scrollback. Drag empty space to pan.

Create where you are

Double-click empty canvas to open a terminal, host, agent or note right there. Hosts can also be dragged in from the left.

Select many, move together

Shift-drag on empty space to box-select, and shift-click to add. Dragging a group's label moves everything inside it.

Tidy, Fit, minimap

Tidy arranges nodes by group in one animated step. Fit frames everything. Click or drag the minimap to move around.

⌥T⌥1 fit ⌥2 focused ⌥0 100%
Wire context by dragging

Drag from the dot on a node's right edge to another node to link context. Links carry an animated flow while the agent works. Click a link's label to remove it.
